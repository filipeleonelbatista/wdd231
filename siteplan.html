PetID Registry — Site Plan

Site Name

PetID Registry — unique digital ID for pets via QR/NFC collar tag. Domain: petid-registry.org

Site Purpose

Central hub for pet QR/NFC medical records, 15-pet adoption/pedigree catalog with filters and modal, and Pet ID registration form with thank-you page.

Scenarios

  • How do I access my pet's vaccines at any vet?
  • Which pets are available for adoption vs pedigree sale and how do I filter them?
